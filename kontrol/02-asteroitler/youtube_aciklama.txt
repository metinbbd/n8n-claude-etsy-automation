Asteroids are 4.5-billion-year-old time capsules of the solar system. This documentary uses real NASA imagery to explore the asteroid belt, Dawn's visits to Vesta and Ceres, the sample OSIRIS-REx brought home from Bennu, the Psyche and Lucy missions, the impacts that have shaped Earth, and DART, humanity's first planetary defense test.
